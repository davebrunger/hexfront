    How to play
    • Select one of your blue units. White hexes show where it can move; red-ringed enemies can be attacked.
    • Each unit can move, then attack once per turn. Attacking ends its move.
    • Attack falls off with range, and targets never fire back.
    • Terrain changes movement cost and reduces damage taken. Hills and forest protect best.
    • Zones of control: entering a hex next to an enemy ends your move.
    • The number above a target shows expected damage dealt.
    • Win by destroying every enemy unit, or by having more strength left after turn 30.
    • Units that stay put recover strength.
    • Esc cancels, N selects the next ready unit, Space ends the turn.
