    How to play
    • Select one of your units. Bright white hexes show where it can move and still attack, faint dashed ones where it can move but not attack; red-ringed enemies can be attacked.
    • Each unit can move, then attack once per turn. Attacking ends its move. Infantry can move 1 space and attack, or 2 without attacking; Armour can move up to 3 and attack; Artillery can attack, or move 1.
    • Attacking rolls one die per point of attack, which falls off with range. Each grenade, or symbol matching the target's type, does 1 damage. Targets never fire back.
    • Terrain: every hex costs 1 space to enter, entering forest, hills or a village ends your move, and lakes and rivers are impassable except at bridges. Attacks on units in forest, on hills or in a village roll one die fewer, except from artillery.
    • Line of sight: every unit except artillery needs a clear line from its hex centre to the target's. Units, forest, villages and the half hexes at the side edges block it; so do hills, unless both units are on hills, and lakes, if the line crosses two lake hexes. The attacker's and target's own hexes never block, and a line running along hex edges is only blocked if both sides are obstructed.
    • Zones of control: entering a hex next to an enemy ends your move.
    • The number above a target shows dice rolled, expected damage and chance to destroy it.
    • Win by destroying every enemy unit, or by having more strength left after turn 30.
    • Esc cancels, N selects the next ready unit, Space ends the turn.
